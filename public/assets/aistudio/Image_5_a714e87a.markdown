## Brand & Style

This design system channels the hyper-energetic, tongue-in-cheek atmosphere of internal corporate superlatives, digital office fan clubs, and meme celebration culture. The aesthetic bridges polished dark-mode SaaS interfaces with unapologetic, playful irreverence—think late-night company award galas meets viral internet award badges.

The visual style blends **Neo-Glassmorphism** with **Vibrant Y2K Meme Energy**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, moody slate canvas illuminated by high-voltage neon glows, sticker-like tilt badges, holographic highlights, and celebratory confetti accents.
- **Tone & Voice:** Self-aware, witty, enthusiastic, and mock-prestigious. 
- **Emotional Response:** Dopamine-inducing delight, laughter, camaraderie, and playful engagement without sacrificing layout utility or readability.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the infamous "Company Polo Emerald," rendered in high-saturation luminosity against a dense, sleek slate night canvas, elevated by high-octane hazard and celebration accents.

- **Primary (`#10B981` Emerald Base, `#059669` Active/Hover, `#064E3B` Tint/Deep Container):** The ceremonial heart of the design, celebrating office champions, trophy counters, and high-energy interactive nodes.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` Warning Orange):** Loud, satirical alerts, urgent "Reply-All" warnings, and high-priority callout triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` Electric Violet):** VIP meme badges, late-night sprint awards, and secondary interactive accents.
- **Highlight Gold (`#FACC15` Trophy Gold):** Confetti highlights, ribbon medals, star ratings, and golden polo pins.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` Slate Dark):** The foundation canvas. Tonal variations include `#1E293B` for elevated panel backing and `#334155` for soft translucent structures.
- **Surface Accents:** Translucent color washes using 12% to 25% opacity with ambient neon edge diffusion.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects a dialogue between bold satirical energy and friendly readability:

- **Headlines (Bricolage Grotesque):** Quirky, expressive, and full of off-kilter personality. Tight tracking and pronounced weights give award titles, meme badges, and mock corporate slogans an authoritative yet comical presence.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Round, clean, and welcoming. Ensures long-form award citations, employee gossip reels, and voting descriptions remain effortlessly legible on dark surfaces.
- **Labels & Micro-data (Space Grotesk):** Geometric, monospaced-adjacent, and technical. Ideal for meme vote counters, timestamp stamps ("Awarded during a 4 PM Friday Deploy"), status pills, and numeric tallies.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a responsive 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) calibrated for dynamic award walls, meme card feeds, and leaderboard grids.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile:** `< 640px` (single-column cards, sticky bottom action bar, 1.25rem margin).
  - **Tablet:** `640px - 1024px` (2-column mosaic layouts, 1.5rem gutters).
  - **Desktop:** `> 1024px` (3 to 4-column dynamic grid, 3rem margins, fixed/staggered heights).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional cadence where content elements remain grouped snugly (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while trophy panels and celebration banners float with generous negative space (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to let neon glows breathe.
- **Tilted Stacking:** Grid items may intentionally feature micro-rotations (-1.5° to +2°) on desktop to evoke sticker albums and desk award clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Neon Glassmorphism** layered directly over the `#0F172A` canvas:

1. **Canvas Layer (Base):** Deep slate `#0F172A` with ambient, multi-color radial gradient blobs (emerald, violet, and orange blurred at 120px with 15% opacity).
2. **Standard Glass Tier (Cards & Panels):**
   - Background: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.65)`.
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.37)`.
3. **Interactive / Hero Tier (Active & Featured Awards):**
   - Background: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`.
   - Neon Edge: 1px to 1.5px solid with vibrant emerald or violet gradients (`#10B981` to `#8B5CF6`).
   - Outer Glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
4. **Floating Popouts (Tooltips, Stickers, Confetti Overlays):**
   - Pure opaque or high-intensity frosted surfaces with dual drop shadows: sharp black structural shadow (`0 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`) combined with an accented ambient neon rim.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) curvature. 

- **Cards & Containers:** Default to 1rem (`rounded-lg`) to balance sleek modern glass surfaces with a touch of toy-like playfulness.
- **Buttons, Badge Pills & Counters:** Employ either standard 0.5rem corner radius or full pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) for high-contrast sticker appeal.
- **Stickers & Ribbons:** Distinct 6-degree or 12-degree chamfers and asymmetrical slant transforms are used selectively on award tag banners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Celebrate / Vote"):** Solid `#10B981` polo green fill with dark text (`#064E3B` or `#0F172A`), bold weight, subtle neon drop glow (`0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`). Scales slightly on hover (`scale(1.03)`).
- **Secondary ("Honorable Mention"):** Translucent dark slate surface with a vibrant `#8B5CF6` electric violet neon border, white text, and a soft violet back-glow on hover.
- **Destructive / Satire Warning ("Overrule HR"):** High-contrast warning orange `#F97316` outline with matching text and a dashed border.

### Award & Trophy Cards
- Glassmorphic panels featuring a 1px continuous luminous border.
- Header contains an emoji or 3D-rendered trophy icon floating above a micro-badge ribbon.
- Interactive footer with real-time reaction counters and a "Sticker Slap" area.

### Badge Stickers & Meme Tags
- Rotated slightly (`rotate-[-2deg]` or `rotate-[3deg]`) to look physically affixed to the screen.
- Thick semi-translucent borders, uppercase `Space Grotesk` typography, and vibrant fills (e.g., `#FACC15` text with `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.15)` background for "Meeting That Could Have Been An Email" awards).

### Meme Counters & Reaction Bars
- Integrated segmented pill bars featuring counter values paired with animated emojis (e.g., green polo shirts, fire, spilled coffee, facepalm).
- Active state lights up with immediate particle ripples and incremental numeric pulse animations.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Glass background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`) with muted placeholder text (`#64748B`).
- On focus: smooth transition into an energetic emerald halo outline (`#10B981`) with inset shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded square containers for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: dark slate with a subtle border. Checked: filled with vivid `#10B981` or `#8B5CF6` displaying high-contrast SVG checkmark or solid dot indicators.
